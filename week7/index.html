Brightwater Studio: a 48-person digital design studio moving into its own office

Brightwater designs apps and websites for public-sector and charity clients. Vision: “A studio clients want to visit and makers want to work in.” After closing the space gap, the brief asks for 36 desks plus the rooms in the kit.

B · Corner building, Level 3: a modern L-shaped floor with floor-to-ceiling glazing onto two streets and onto a planted roof terrace in the inner corner (double doors from both arms). Lifts, stair and WCs in a concrete core at the inner corner; concrete columns just inside the glazing; long arms with views at both ends.

You will make two layouts of the same floor and compare them. Choose a building above, then follow steps 1–4. Drag items from the kit; click an item for ⟳ rotate and ✕ delete (or press R, also while dragging). Draw walls along the grid lines and leave a gap for a door.
