1 · From headcount to desks

Future headcount = now × (1 + growth), rounded to a whole person. Growth can be negative: the team gets smaller. Peak demand = future headcount × peak utilisation (1 decimal). Desks = your decision: peak demand plus a buffer, or fewer if you share more.

    Model answer · worked example: Fieldstone Consulting (growth 25% · 10% · 50%)

    The correct calculation does not fit

    With a 10% buffer: 15 + 15 + 14 desks, plus the PA and the director’s office. With all the support space, the total is about 490 m², about 90 m² more than the floor.

    Three ways to close the gap

    • Share more desks: the 60% team 15 → 12 bookable; the 35% team 14 → 10 touchdown (about 35 m²).
    • Make rooms work harder: the training room doubles as a large meeting room; drop two 8-person rooms (about 40 m²).
    • Smaller support space: two focus rooms and four booths instead of three focus rooms.
    • Or ask for more space: +90 m² ≈ 970 sq ft; at a practice rate of £70/sq ft, about £68,000 a year more.

    Your growth rates are different, so your numbers will differ. Every option costs the users something: a strong answer chooses, says what users lose, and how it will be managed.
